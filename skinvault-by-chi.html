Why a vault

The internet has ten thousand products. You need about twelve.

Most skincare fails melanin-rich skin in the same few ways — it fades unevenly, it triggers dark marks that outlast the breakout, or it was simply never tested on tones like ours. SkinVault exists to end the trial-and-error.

Every product here has been chosen for how it behaves on deeper skin, in real heat and humidity, at prices that make sense in naira. You’re not buying more. You’re buying the right ones.

Q.1

Does it respect melanin-rich skin?

We favour ingredients with a track record on deeper tones — niacinamide, tranexamic acid, azelaic acid, vitamin C, gentle retinoids — and flag the ones that need care.

Q.2

Does the ingredient list earn the price?

We read the formula, not the marketing. A serum earns its naira on what’s inside, not on the box.

Q.3

Will it survive Nigerian weather?

Texture, finish and stability in heat and humidity are part of the test. A cream that pills at 32 degrees doesn’t make it.

Q.4

Can we explain it in one honest sentence?

If we can’t tell you plainly what a product does and who it’s for, it doesn’t belong here.
